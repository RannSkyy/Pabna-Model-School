Our Heritage & Identity

About Pabna Model School

Providing transformative education rooted in moral excellence, critical thinking, and progressive values for over six decades.

A Beacon of Quality Education in North Bengal

Founded in 1962, Pabna Model School has continuously strived to deliver holistic, student-centered education. Over the past six decades, thousands of alumni have graduated to become prominent doctors, engineers, civil servants, researchers, entrepreneurs, and global leaders.

We integrate the rigorous National Curriculum with state-of-the-art STEM laboratories, co-curricular arts, sports, and value-based discipline.

Our Vision

To be the premier educational institution in Bangladesh, inspiring students toward academic mastery, ethical leadership, and creative innovation.

Our Mission

To cultivate intellectual curiosity, character integrity, social responsibility, and resilience in a supportive, technologically advanced environment.

"Education is not merely the accumulation of facts, but the training of the mind to think, empathize, and lead with unwavering courage."
Mohammad Abdur Rahman Principal, Pabna Model School

Academic Programs

Primary Education

Class 1 – Class 5

Campus Facilities

Providing modern amenities, safe environments, and advanced resources to support learning, athletics, and student well-being.

Modern Library

20,000+ Books & Periodicals

Spacious, air-conditioned reading halls featuring rare archives, academic reference manuals, national dailies, and digital e-readers connected to global online academic libraries.

  • Quiet study & research zones
  • Automated digital cataloging
  • Dedicated primary children's corner

Science Laboratories

Physics, Chemistry & Biology

Three independent, well-ventilated laboratories stocked with state-of-the-art apparatus, precision digital balances, optical microscopes, and comprehensive safety containment systems.

  • Individual workbench equipment sets
  • Certified lab assistants & safety gear
  • Interactive chemical demonstration unit

Computer Lab

100+ High-Performance PCs

Dual state-of-the-art multimedia computer centers fitted with fiber-optic gigabit internet, licensed educational software, coding compilers (Python/C++), and interactive smart screens.

  • 100+ Intel Core i5 work stations
  • High-speed fiber optic connection
  • Coding and robotics clubs

Sports Complex

Cricket, Football & Badminton

A 3-acre lush green athletic playground featuring international-standard turf cricket practice nets, football pitch, basketball court, and indoor table tennis/badminton arena.

  • Certified fitness & physical instructors
  • Annual sports meet & inter-house cups
  • Changing rooms and spectator galleries

Grand Auditorium

500-Seat Acoustic Hall

Air-conditioned multipurpose auditorium with high-definition stage lighting, pro acoustic surround sound, and backstage green rooms hosting cultural events, seminars, and debates.

  • Surround audio & stage spotlights
  • Centrally air-conditioned comfort
  • Hosts annual national conferences

Campus Medical Center

Full-Time Health Station

On-campus health center staffed by a registered physician and certified nurse, offering immediate first-aid, emergency triage, routine student health checkups, and wellness monitoring.

  • 24/7 on-campus first aid standby
  • Annual student ophthalmology & ENT tests
  • Emergency hospital tie-up in Pabna

Admission Process 2027

A transparent, straightforward admission journey designed to welcome motivated young minds to Pabna Model School.

01

Online Application

Fill out the digital admission form below or collect a physical application kit from our campus office.

02

Document Review

Submit birth certificate, previous school academic transcripts, and passport photos for verification.

03

Assessment & Interview

A friendly interactive evaluation to assess basic literacy, numeracy, and an informal parental meet.

04

Enrollment & Welcome

Complete enrollment formalities, receive the student ID card, uniform kit, and academic calendar.

Key Admission Highlights

We welcome prospective learners irrespective of background, striving to provide financial stipends to exceptionally meritorious applicants.

Eligibility Class 1 minimum age 6+ years as of Jan 1, 2027. Class 6–11 requires relevant previous grade clearance.
Key Dates Applications Close: December 15, 2026. Evaluation Tests: December 20, 2026.
Required Documents Birth Registration Certificate (Online verified), 3 passport photos, and last grade marksheet.
Scholarships Available Up to 100% tuition waivers for GPA 5.00 holders and national talent contenders.
